# MVP scope

## Included

The first meaningful public version contains:

1. Homepage and responsive shared layout.
2. A small, validated, versioned sample game dataset.
3. Searchable and filterable unit catalogue.
4. Unit detail pages with practical explanations.
5. Division-builder grid and support slots.
6. Simplified, transparent role-based calculations and evaluation.
7. Structured nation-guide system.
8. One complete example nation path.
9. About page and visible fan-project disclaimer.
10. Automated tests, documentation, and continuous integration.

## Explicitly excluded

- Authentication and profiles.
- Saving and publishing community content.
- Ratings, comments, reports, and moderation.
- Automated news or video scraping.
- A complete game-data catalogue.
- Every nation or political path.
- Exact simulation of every HOI4 mechanic.
- Tank designer, doctrine simulator, and multiplayer meta system.
- Production deployment infrastructure beyond a simple documented path.

## Quality bar

The MVP should be understandable, version-aware, accessible, responsive, and honest about simplified calculations. A feature is not complete until its relevant tests and production build pass and its documentation is current.
