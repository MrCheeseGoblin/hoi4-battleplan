# Product vision

HOI4 Battleplan turns raw Hearts of Iron IV data and scattered strategy advice into clear, practical decisions.

## Audience

- Newer players who need explanations rather than unexplained numbers.
- Experienced players who want compact, patch-aware reference material.
- Community authors who may later publish templates and guides.

## Product pillars

1. **Division coaching** — build a template, select its intended role, and receive a transparent evaluation.
2. **Practical unit catalogue** — browse canonical unit data alongside strengths, weaknesses, requirements, and typical uses.
3. **Actionable nation guides** — follow structured paths covering focuses, research, production, divisions, timing, and common mistakes.
4. **Community knowledge, later** — publish and discuss versioned templates only after the core tools and moderation foundations are trustworthy.
5. **Curated updates, later** — link to official patch notes, developer diaries, selected videos, and important site changes without broad scraping.

## Principles

- One canonical source of truth for mechanical game data.
- Clear separation between game facts, Battleplan recommendations, and community opinion.
- Patch and game-version context wherever information can become outdated.
- Useful without an account.
- Honest assumptions and uncertainty.
- Compact, accessible, original interface design.

## Positioning

HOI4 Battleplan is not another wall-of-text wiki and not only a stat calculator. Its purpose is to explain what a player can do with the data and why a choice fits a particular role or situation.

## Disclaimer

HOI4 Battleplan is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Paradox Interactive. Official game assets will not be used without permission.
